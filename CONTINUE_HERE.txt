4. CURRENT PICKUP BEHAVIOUR

Detection and height:
  - SAM 3 provides matching regions/masks. It does not return metric object depth.
  - Named-object prompt retries and closer table crops help recover small or
    sideways objects. No generic "any small object" wrist fallback is used.
  - small_object_grasp.py contains profiles for "small white cylinder" and
    "small black cap". Cylinder silhouette fitting compares upright and sideways
    hypotheses using the known 2 x 1 cm dimensions and approximate camera geometry.
  - An accepted upright cylinder fit selects a closing height of 1 cm above table.
  - An accepted sideways cylinder fit selects 0.5 cm.
  - Ambiguous/rejected cylinder geometry uses a LABELLED 0.5 cm fallback.
  - Cap remains at a LABELLED 0.5 cm range-based fallback. Its height is NOT
    automatically measured. At exactly 5 mm tall it is at this clearance boundary.
  - The fit must agree with the standing aim within 2 cm and be reachable. Height
    selection has additional overlap, hypothesis-separation, mask-size and
    frame-clipping checks. These are heuristics, not measured confidence guarantees.
  - Other objects retain the older upright-height estimate: half estimated height,
    clamped to 1.5–6 cm; unknown height falls back to 1.5 cm. Arbitrary thin,
    sideways, irregular or elevated objects are NOT reliably supported by this.

Wrist centring:
  - Both cameras are enabled by default; --hand makes that explicit.
  - The current wrist target point is the mask's pixel centroid compared with a
    SAVED target pixel in controller/calib/hand.json. It does not detect the actual
    physical midpoint of the claws anew in each image.
  - hand.json supplies a 2D pixels-per-cm matrix and a target pixel, not full 3D
    camera-to-gripper calibration. A wrong saved target can remain wrong even when
    the terminal says alignment is confirmed.
  - Distant detections are filtered before selection. Local cropped retries keep
    the requested identity; old arbitrary "small white object" substitution was
    removed from the active search path.
  - The two small-object profiles allow corrections up to 0.5 cm per step, with
    total suggested displacement limited to 2 cm from the standing aim.
  - Cap and sideways/uncertain cylinder use fine centring: estimated offset under
    0.15 cm in two fresh images, agreeing within 0.1 cm, with at most eight looks.
    No extra arm move is made between the two confirmation observations. Repeated
    host frame IDs do not count twice. This is NOT proven 1.5 mm physical accuracy.
  - Bottle and confidently upright cylinder retain their earlier 0.3 cm / one-look
    acceptance, with at most five alignment looks.
  - If wrist alignment is missing, lost or unconfirmed, the code still proceeds
    using the standing aim. This is a fallback, NOT wrist-confirmed centring.
  - The small-object profiles have no inherited 1 cm outward offset. Other objects
    retain that fallback offset unless wrist alignment is confirmed.

Descent and outcome:
  - Normal pickup honours its planned numeric closing height. It does NOT descend
    until table contact is mandatory. Do not restore automatic feel_all_the_way.
  - Controller descent tapers carried-over shoulder/sag compensation to zero
    toward the closing endpoint and avoids a fresh sag push immediately before
    closing. It retains guarded contact handling and complete-path requirements.
  - Wrist alignment happens at a hover pose. There is NO continuous camera
    correction during the final descent and NO general 3D grasp planner here.
  - Holding is inferred from gripper encoder behaviour, with a slip check after
    the normal top-pick lift. It is not visual proof of the correct object's pickup.
  - Latest terminal labels: HOLD DETECTED, MISSED, SLIPPED, FAILED, UNCONFIRMED.
    Previously OK could be printed with holding:false because only request
    completion was checked. cc63f63 fixes reporting, not physical accuracy.
